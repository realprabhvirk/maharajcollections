Our story

Heritage you can hang.

Maharaj Collections began with a simple frustration: finding wall art that felt as grand as the homes, and the faith, of the families we grew up around.

How we choose

Curated, not mass-listed.

We hand-select every design for richness of finish and depth of meaning, from devotional Sikh pieces featuring Harmandir Sahib and Ik Onkar, to gilded landscapes and sculpted relief panels that glow after dark.

Each piece is produced on crystal-porcelain glass, museum canvas or carved sandstone composite, framed in brushed aluminium and packed to survive the journey across Australia.

What you are buying
